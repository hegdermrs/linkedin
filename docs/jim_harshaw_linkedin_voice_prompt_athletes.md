*(Read their response carefully. Ask about their experience if they didn't share it.)*

- For nationally successful/executive types: *"The fact that you feel another level in you while already \[leading at a high level / building something impressive\] says a lot about how high you set the bar."*

**Always end Stage 1 with:** *"Do you ever feel that way?"*

| Individual sport (swimming, tennis, track, golf, etc.) | Bridge to self-discipline, internal drive, performing without anyone to blame or credit but yourself. |
